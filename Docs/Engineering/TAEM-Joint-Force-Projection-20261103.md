# Coupled projection onto the relaxed normal-force envelope

NormalForceEnvelope.projectJointly computes the closest requested (vertical, lateral) acceleration vector in the relaxed positive-lift sector with the caller's existing maximum lift and bank bounds. Both residual components have units m/s²; the squared Euclidean residual treats those physical axes alike. It does not reserve desired vertical support first.

For requested angle atan2(lateral, vertical), project the angle onto the configured sector. On that ray, project requested force onto the ray and bound its magnitude to [0, maximumLift]. The resulting lift/bank pair generates both acceleration components together. There are no gains, time thresholds, altered vehicle capability or scenario branches. Nonfinite/negative capability bounds and bank sectors outside this positive-vertical convention are unavailable. The existing vertical-first allocate method and all live callers are unchanged.

This relaxed set is an instantaneous force bound, not a complete attainable-command/mission model. The actual polar may not realize every magnitude between zero and the maximum at allowed incidence/trim. Aerodynamic inversion and forward evaluation must retain that residual, plus drag, q/load, finite pitch/roll/actuator response, original evidence support and the common terminal continuation. Projection alone neither certifies descent/terrain safety nor prices the physical consequences of sacrificing support. Euclidean projection is a transparent seed construction, not an established mission-outcome ranking or permission to replace guidance without validation.

One focused check passed using the recorded STS state, unchanged configuration, actual configured bank/load limits and the existing aerodynamic model's maximum lift. A conflicting requested vector at that modeled lift bound gives zero lateral force under vertical-first allocation. Joint projection retains lateral force with lower requested-force residual and lower vertical support, while preserving the same total lift/bank bounds; mirrored requests give mirrored results and nonfinite requests are unavailable. Only the requested vector changes; physical capability is not scaled. No trajectory performance experiment or landing claim is made.
