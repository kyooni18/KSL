# Motion-derived course work for command candidates

TAEMCourseProgress records sampled ground-course change in the fixed selected-runway chart, positive right. Command schedule prefixes now retain it alongside propagated motion and accounting. It accumulates signed course change and total absolute change independently of any proposed HAC center, radius or winding label. Therefore moving a proposal cannot itself spend this course-work diagnostic.

It is not spiral polar phase, a full winding-state/rebase implementation, or proof of a dynamically realized spiral. Changing the selected runway requires another frame/context. The prefix tracker starts at that prefix's seed; a measured-state replan must separately retain/transform the prior progress history. Reinitializing this tracker must not later be interpreted as completing or discarding old winding. The existing live TAEM tracker is unchanged.

Stationary or numerically unresolved horizontal course, nonmonotonic/inconsistent same-time samples, a sample gap beyond the explicitly supplied cadence coverage, and exactly ambiguous half-turn samples stop coverage. A later valid sample cannot restore missing turns. Known accumulated work and throughTime are retained at the last accepted sample. Duplicate identical same-time samples add no progress. Command prefixes use configured maximumSnapshotSpan as declared observation-gap coverage, not as a physical angular-rate bound. Timestamp ulps provide only subtraction-roundoff allowance.

The relative sqrt(machine-epsilon) horizontal-projection guard addresses course singularity near radial/zero velocity; it is not a minimum airspeed or turning-authority gate. Sampled unwrapping still assumes adequate cadence: more than a half-turn between accepted samples can alias. This checkpoint does not prove that sampling bound for arbitrary flight states or replace full continuous winding reconstruction. Course chart curvature includes the fixed runway projection and must not be confused with body yaw or polar spiral progress.
