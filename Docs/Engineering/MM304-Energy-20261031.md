## Demonstrated baseline faults

`entry-energy-probe --input LOG --configuration LOG.configuration.json` resets
the native plant to each recorded initial state and flies the **recorded stick
commands**, not new guidance. CSV evidence is in
`runs/mm304-energy-investigation-20261031/baseline.csv`. Specific energy is
fixed-frame kinetic energy plus gravitational and centrifugal potential
(J/kg); energy height uses runway potential and runway gravity, not an ASL
height mixed with an AGL target. Drag work is trapezoidal D/m times airspeed
times elapsed simulation time. Gaps over a second are excluded, not invented.

Altitude-to-speed exchange is **not** an energy loss. The agreement between
measured mechanical-energy loss and drag work establishes aerodynamic loss.
The shuttle holds almost 40° AoA from +100 to +320 s; target bank reaches 70°
by +132 s despite lift being much less than the vertical demand. This is not
a required S turn: the lateral law starts at the maximum energy bank, and its
infeasible-crossrange branch **increases** bank when the vertical demand
already exceeds all lift. It can also integrate its bank beyond maximumBank.
That sends the vehicle deeper into dense atmosphere and extends its path.

The capture budget is merely applied to referenceDrag, not the final AoA:
inversion is floored at best-L/D AoA, negative modulation is discarded by
`max(baseAlpha, baseAlpha + modulation)`, and positive modulation, q protection,
and stored dragBoost can all raise AoA again. At +400 s D/m is 13.37 m/s² vs
reference 6.31; at +420 it is 10.97 vs 3.24; at +440 reference is **zero** but
target AoA remains 20.3°. Continued best-L/D flight does not magically deliver
the unavailable capture energy. Vertical recovery must also be priced.

The plant mismatch is separate and demonstrated, not inferred from guidance:
for +360–400 s median modeled pitch acceleration under the same commands is
−0.493 rad/s², while measured acceleration is approximately zero. At +400–440
it is −1.009 vs −0.002 rad/s². RotationalDynamicsEngine applies the inherited
subsonic stiffness everywhere, while FlightController explicitly refuses that
unsupported high-Mach moment prior. Native `observe` also replaces measured
total actuator authority by q-only surfaces; native `advance` never applies
command.rcsEnabled to systems.rcs. This is not a force-polar-only discrepancy.
Independent ShuttleSim retains its explicitly configured direct-moment model;
its source, tables and moments must not be changed to make guidance pass.

Baseline has not demonstrated a runway touchdown or stop. At +700 s it is
still MM304, about 7.7 km ASL, Mach 0.32, nearly out of range and far below
capture energy. Stopped the existing fly process before rebuilding.
